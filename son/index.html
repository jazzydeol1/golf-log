🏃 Fitness & football

⚽ Football and your other training come first. On a heavy football week, skip both fitness sessions and just do the three golf sessions.

Two short sessions a week, on any days. Bodyweight and a resistance band only. Stop if anything hurts. The weekly plan (✅ This week) steps the reps up each block and has a score box for each one.

🏃 F1 · Mobility25 min
Hips, upper back and shoulders. Do 2 rounds (3 from January).
  1. Warm-up: 2 min marching on the spot, then 10 big arm circles each way
  2. 90/90 hip switches: sit with both knees bent to one side, swing them to the other side, 6–12 each side
  3. Lunge and twist: back knee down, turn your chest over your front knee, 4–10 each side
  4. Open books: lie on your side, knees bent, open your top arm like a book, 6–12 each side
  5. Band pull-aparts: band straight out in front, pull it to your chest, 10–16 reps
  6. Club turns: club across your shoulders in golf stance, turn back and through slowly, 6–12 each way
  7. Finish: deep squat test: heels flat, chest up, hold up to 60 s. ✏️ Score = best hold (seconds)
💡 Feel: Move slowly and breathe out on every stretch: a stretch, never pain.
🏃 F2 · Core & rotation25 min
Planks, side planks and band rotations. Do 2 rounds (3 from January), 1 min rest between rounds.
  1. Front plank: forearms and toes, body straight like a board, 20–40 s
  2. Side plank: on one forearm, hips up (knees down is fine), 15–30 s each side
  3. Dead bugs: on your back, straighten opposite arm and leg slowly, 8–12 each side
  4. Band rotations: side-on to a band at chest height, arms straight, turn your chest away and back, hips quiet, 8–12 each side
  5. Glute bridges: feet flat, push your hips up and squeeze, 10–14 reps
  6. Finish: plank test: hold with good form, stop at 2 min. ✏️ Score = seconds held
💡 Feel: Squeeze your tummy like someone is about to poke it, and keep breathing.
